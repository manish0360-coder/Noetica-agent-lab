# H31 — Vertical Realization may not be the universal computational principle.

Status:
Working hypothesis.

Reason:
Engineering maps abstract representations toward concrete realizations, but perception performs the opposite mapping (concrete → abstract).

Therefore the deeper invariant may be:

Representation Transformation

rather than

Abstraction → Realization.
